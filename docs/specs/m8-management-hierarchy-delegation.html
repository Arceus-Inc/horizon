Why M8 is the structural unlock

Today Horizon chooses a goal and Chorus executes a task, but the handoff still compresses a company into one assignee. M8 preserves the proven delegation mechanics while replacing the generic Manager identity with specialist leadership.

Goals and non-goals

Goals
  • Composable, bounded, auditable management authority.
  • Explicit task modes with no craft/management leakage.
  • Direct-report teams and explicitly granted nested delegation.
  • One accountable lead and independent parent verification.
  • Horizon multi-task aggregation and capacity-aware priority.
  • Exact-once fan-out, bounded beats, recovery, and live proof.
Non-goals
  • Autonomous promotions or reporting-line changes.
  • Cross-line borrowing or matrix reporting in M8.
  • Craft work hidden in a delegation parent beat.
  • Horizon choosing people or authoring task topology.
  • A second scheduler or capacity reservation system.
  • Silent conversion of stale Manager employees.

Profession

What craft can this employee perform?

Authority

Assignment

Which durable group owns this work?

Is this craft delivery or coordination?

Task execution mode

class ExecutionMode(StrEnum):
    DELIVERY = "delivery"      # profession tools, DoD, and lander
    DELEGATION = "delegation"  # coordination-only management contract

Existing tasks default to delivery. A Team root and explicitly granted area children use delegation. Child creation cannot retroactively change a delivery task's mode.

DelegationContract

One row per delegation task pins the Team, accountable lead, management-profile version, parent contract, subdelegation grant, effective depth/team/spend limits, objective rubric, state, and acceptance evidence.

RD-1 · Team lifetime decision

M8 implements a per-goal mission Team. The standing-team alternative remains documented because both policies share the same Team, membership, contract, tool, and scheduler interfaces.

Rejected alternative

Reusable standing team

A named squad survives individual goals. New work selects an existing eligible team after current policy validation.

Strengths

  • Stable identity, memory, and norms.
  • Less repeated formation work.
  • Natural product-area ownership.
  • Clear portfolio reporting by squad.

Risks

  • Membership can become stale.
  • Less dynamic staffing.
  • May duplicate the line org.
  • Whole-team queues can hide overload.
Selected for M8

Per-goal mission team

A durable Team is formed for one delegated root, lives through integration, and archives with the parent outcome.

Strengths

  • Composition follows the work.
  • Clean one-goal audit boundary.
  • No long-lived membership drift.
  • Strong fit with the current parent lifecycle.

Risks

  • Repeated formation cost.
  • Weaker durable team identity.
  • More Team rows.
  • Frequent reshuffling may reduce coherence.

Execution profile resolution

ExecutionProfileResolver.resolve(employee, task)
    |
    +-- task.mode == delivery
    |      -> profession manifest + profession DoD + profession lander
    |
    +-- task.mode == delegation
           -> validate ManagementProfile + DelegationContract
           -> validate employee == contract lead == Team lead
           -> sealed management tools + Team memory
           -> delegated verifier + subtree lander
delivery

Profession contract

Engineer still gets engineer tools, reviewed-build DoD, and PR lander. Designer still gets design tools, design DoD, and design artifact. Holding a management profile changes nothing.

delegation

Coordination contract

Read goal/team/outcomes, decompose once, then accept or make one bounded reaction. No craft write/run tools. Independent parent verification follows lead acceptance.

First vertical proofThe same management-capable engineer receives one delivery task and one delegation task. Tools, DoDs, and landers must differ exactly; any leakage falsifies the architecture.
8

Delegation lifecycle

1 · Root intake and lead selection

Horizon sends requirements, priority, and optional user override. Chorus validates or deterministically selects a profiled specialist with legal report coverage and budget headroom.

2 · Kickoff and Team formation

The lead sees filtered direct reports with observed load, limits, and reusable Team candidates. One exact-once decompose call creates the wave.

3 · Delivery and nested delegation

Delivery children use profession contracts. Delegated area children require a management-capable direct report plus an explicit subdelegation grant.

4 · Park, wake, react

The parent releases its beat while children work. children_done wakes a fresh lead session with Team, child, artifact, blocker, and load evidence.

5 · Accept, independently verify, land

The lead accepts or makes one corrective move. Acceptance enters verification; only descendant closure + lead acceptance + independent objective pass lands the subtree.

Child wave rules

  • Every child declares label + intent + assignee + depends_on + execution_mode.
  • The lead cannot assign themselves, a peer, skip-level report, or read-only reviewer.
  • Nested delegation requires both profile permission and task/Team grant.
  • The full wave is validated before any Team or child write.
  • Repeated calls reuse the same claim, Team memberships, and child IDs.
No force-success escapeIntegrate-iteration exhaustion opens recovery or human escalation. It never mechanically accepts a subtree that failed its objective gate.

Reorganisation guard

While a contract is active, changes to affected reporting lines, employee termination, pinned profile authority, or required standing-team membership raise ActiveDelegationConflict and write nothing.

Migration from the stale Manager employee

A role="manager" row does not reveal whether the person is fundamentally an engineer, designer, PM, or analyst. Migration requires an explicit profession.

chorus workforce specialize-manager <employee_id> \
    --profession engineer \
    --profile <policy>
  1. Validate the target specialist plugin.
  2. Reject migration while affected work is active.
  3. Update profession and create the management profile atomically.
  4. Preserve identity, reporting edges, budget, memory, history, and audit lineage.
  5. Fail fast on unmigrated Manager rows; never guess a profession.

The profession-neutral management brief, verifier, contract resolver, and subtree landing move into Chorus management modules. Model-callable tools remain in chorus_tools.

  1. Every delegated task has exactly one accountable lead and active contract.
  2. Every lead is a specialist with a compatible active management profile.
  3. Every M8 child assignee is the lead's direct report.
  4. Every management mutation intersects profile, line, Team, parent, and task authority.
  5. Delivery and delegation surfaces never coexist in one parent beat.
  6. Completion requires descendants, lead acceptance, and independent verification.

  8. Existing beat and budget gates remain runtime capacity authority.
  9. Fan-out is exact-once and audit-visible.
  10. No bounded-loop escape path may manufacture success.

Live capstone · a real company loop

Org

  • No employee with profession manager.
  • One management-capable specialist root lead.
  • At least two direct reports with different professions.
  • One management-capable report with their own reports.
  • One independent reviewer outside deliverable ownership.

Scenario

  1. CEO approves a Horizon decision.
  2. Horizon creates a team-shaped goal with no named assignee.
  3. Chorus selects the lead; a second run proves user override.
  4. The lead forms/selects a Team and delegates whole areas.
  5. A specialist report sub-delegates one area.
  6. Profession-specific children land their artifacts.
  7. An injected child failure forces one bounded reaction.
  8. The root lead accepts the assembled result.
  9. Independent verification passes only after correction.
  10. Horizon folds the subtree and explains effective priority.

Monitor every action

Capture every beat, wake, run, tool surface, tool input/result, Team/member/contract/task write, authorization decision, DoD verdict, worktree landing, capacity input, and Horizon fold. Assert zero unauthorized successful mutations, duplicates, orphan tasks, unresolved contracts, manifest errors, or false-positive root completions.

17

Definition of done

  • One specialist identity executes delivery and delegation tasks with no contract leakage.
  • No default workforce role named manager is required.
  • Management authority is bounded, versioned, human-governed, and audited.
  • Durable Team membership cannot widen the line hierarchy.
  • Root and nested delegation are exact-once and depth bounded.
  • Delegation parents contain no hidden craft work.
  • Lead acceptance and independent verification gate completion.
  • Loop exhaustion escalates and never force-passes.
  • Active work blocks affected reorganisation atomically.
  • Horizon supplies capabilities, never people or task topology.
  • Multi-task goals close only on the verified root outcome.
  • Capacity reuses beat/task/budget facts and adds no scheduler.
  • Manager migration is explicit and preserves identity/history.
  • Deterministic, adversarial, crash/retry, cross-repo, and repeated live tests pass.
