Horizon · the strategy layer · capability report

What Horizon can do now

Horizon turns a leader's strategic decision into measurable goals, gets them executed, and keeps steering as real results land — so a decision doesn't die in a doc, it ships.

The spine: Decision → Goal → Task.  dream completes one task · chorus runs the org that ships a sprint · horizon decides what the company should do next — and closes the loop when the work lands.

The gap it closes

Before horizon

A strategy decision ("grow profit by concentrating sales investment") lands as a paragraph. Someone hand-translates it into tasks, guesses priorities, and there is no thread back from "the work finished" to "was the strategy right." Direction drifts silently.

With horizon

The decision is a first-class object. Horizon decomposes it into concrete, measurable goals, submits them for execution at the right priority, and folds every real verdict back into a live picture of which goals are on track, drifting, or blocked — and re-attempts the ones that fail.

Capabilities today — the product

Decompose

Turn a decision into measurable goals

Give horizon a strategic decision in plain language. It uses an LLM to break it into a handful of concrete goals — each with a title, metric, target, and rationale — not vague intentions.

Why it matters: strategy becomes executable and measurable on day one, with no manual translation step.

Grounded

Stay grounded in what the org actually has

Decomposition is anchored to a supplied context (the real data, systems, and constraints), so horizon won't invent goals that depend on data sources or capabilities that don't exist.

Why it matters: goals are achievable, not aspirational fiction — the team never chases work it can't do.

Prioritise

Rank and submit for execution

Every goal gets a score that maps to an execution priority (high / medium / low). Horizon submits the ranked goals straight into the execution layer as ready-to-run work.

Why it matters: the most valuable work rises to the top automatically, and the strategy layer and the delivery layer stay in sync.

Idempotent

Never duplicate work

Each goal carries a stable fingerprint. Re-running the loop, retrying, or re-submitting never spawns a second copy of the same task.

Why it matters: safe to run continuously — no accidental double-staffing or duplicated spend.

Close the loop

Learn from real outcomes

When work lands with a real pass / fail verdict, horizon folds it back in: the goal's health and score update and its priority is rewritten. Every event is accounted for — nothing is silently dropped.

Why it matters: direction is driven by what actually shipped, not by opinion — the loop is a measurable system, not a conversation.

Health & drift

Know what's on track, drifting, or blocked

Horizon maintains a health model per goal (on track / drifting / blocked) from its pass rate, and a staleness sweep quietly loses trust in goals last verified long ago — resurfacing them before they rot.

Why it matters: problems surface early and old "green" goals can't hide stale.

Self-heal

Recover failed goals automatically

A goal that fails is re-opened as a fresh attempt that carries why it failed into the next run, and tells the worker its earlier files, scripts, and outputs are still in the workspace — so each retry builds on the last instead of starting blind. The loop repeats until the goal is done or attempts run out.

Why it matters: the system pushes goals to completion end-to-end instead of leaving a trail of blocked work.

Direction

A live read of the whole decision

At any moment, ask horizon for the current direction: every goal with its score, priority, health, status, and the task realizing it — a single, honest picture of where the sprint stands.

Why it matters: leaders get an always-current status without chasing updates.

Transparency

Full-transparency reporting

Horizon can emit a report that shows every decision it made and why — the raw prompt and LLM output, the exact score→priority and health arithmetic, the real verdicts, the deliverable the worker produced (e.g. a findings.md), and a per-employee tools & operations log (which tools were used, how many calls, errors, tokens, time).

Why it matters: the strategy loop is fully auditable — you can see exactly how a decision turned into shipped work.

Who owns what

LayerOwnsHorizon's relationship
horizon Strategy — decisions, decomposition, ranking, outcome policy, health & direction. This library. Owns the "what" and "why."
chorus Execution — schedules and runs the org's beats, lands artifacts and DoD verdicts. A sibling. Horizon submits goals to it and reads its verdicts — never imports or schedules it.
dream The agent loop — completes a single task with tools inside one worktree. Two layers down. Horizon never runs it; it only observes the outcome.

The rule: horizon owns the decision and never rewrites it. When a goal struggles, its levers are the worker's brief, tools, and priority — the "how" stays with the worker.

Under the hood — the tech

  • Ports & adapters. Horizon binds only to three typed Protocols — GoalStore, IntakePort, OutcomeFeed — plus a minimal Reasoner. A single composition root wires it to a real backend; the core stays pure and swappable.
  • Structured LLM outputs. Anything the system parses (goal decomposition) uses strict json_schema responses, with a tolerant fallback — verified live against Azure OpenAI gpt-5.2.
  • Model-agnostic. The reasoner is just a complete() seam — a real substrate, a fake, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint all work the same.
  • Observable by construction. Every landed event is classified handled / dropped / deferred and exposed via listener_stats() — the wiring proves it's live instead of failing silently.
  • Its own store. Strategy state (decisions + per-goal strategy records) lives in horizon's own .horizon/ stores keyed by goal id — it never mutates the execution layer's data model.
  • Quality bar. Type-safe under mypy --strict, ruff clean, and 77 passing tests including a public-API pin, integration tests, and a live capstone.

What you can run today

CLI

Offline, over horizon's own stores

# seed a decision, then inspect direction
uv run horizon seed "Grow profit by focusing sales investment"
uv run horizon decisions
uv run horizon show
SDK

Drive the full loop in code

horizon.seed_decision(decision)
horizon.decompose(decision.id)     # LLM → goals
horizon.submit_decision(decision.id)
horizon.start()                    # outcomes → health + re-priority
horizon.recover()                  # retry failed goals
horizon.sweep_staleness()          # resurface stale goals
horizon.state()                    # the live direction
